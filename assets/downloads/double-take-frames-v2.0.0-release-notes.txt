DOUBLE TAKE FRAMES DESIGN RELEASE 2.0.0
Released 2026-08-31

Coordinated main-frame, capstan, and clock-string-guide release.

CHANGES
- Promoted the latest water-cassette geometry as the shared main-frame source.
  Its saved default is the dry configuration: dry = true and wet = false.
- Introduced the captive capstans and matching clock-string guide.
- Added a combined OpenSCAD controller for the capstan and guide set.
- Rebuilt both prepared 3MF projects with the new meshes while retaining their
  established nozzle profiles and support enforcers.
- Carried the dry-wall rig, frame stand, latch and keeper, and roller geometry
  forward unchanged into the complete 2.0.0 release set.

COMPATIBILITY
The 2.0.0 capstans and clock-string guide are a coordinated pair. Do not mix
either part with its 1.x counterpart.

KNOWN DOCUMENTATION NOTE
The general how-it-works video may still show the earlier weight mechanism. The
current written assembly instructions govern.

VERSIONING
This project follows Semantic Versioning 2.0.0: https://semver.org/
MAJOR = incompatible printed-part or hardware interface change.
MINOR = backward-compatible new part, option, or substantial improvement.
PATCH = backward-compatible geometry, tolerance, documentation, or packaging fix.

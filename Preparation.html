Before assembly

Images

You need a Cover Image and a Reveal Image. If your order includes prepared images, they arrive formatted, printed, and cut.

Prepared images

Keep both images with the parts and continue to the assembly guide.

Preparing the images yourself?

Use Make 5x7 to position a PNG for the frame and print it at the correct size on standard 8.5 × 11 paper.

Have ready: A single-hole punch for making the slot in the Cover Image.

Images close to a 5x7 shape work best. Leave extra room around important details, especially near the edges, so the final crop still works in the frame.

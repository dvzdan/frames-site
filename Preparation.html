Step 1

Prepare the images

Everyone assembling a frame needs a Cover Image and a Reveal Image. Prepare and print your own, or skip this step if your order includes printed-and-cut images.

Preparing your own?

Use Make 5x7 to position a PNG for the frame and print it at the correct size on standard 8.5 × 11 paper.

Have ready: A single-hole punch for making the slot in the Cover Image.

Images close to a 5x7 shape work best. Leave extra room around important details, especially near the edges, so the final crop still works in the frame.

Open Make 5x7

Ordered prepared images?

Your Cover and Reveal Images arrive formatted, printed, and cut. Continue directly to the parts check below.

Prepare your images

Use Make 5x7 to position any PNG for a 5x7 frame and print it correctly on standard 8.5x11 paper.

Images close to a 5x7 shape work best. Other proportions can usually be adapted, but leave extra room around anything important, especially near the edges, so the final crop still works in the frame.

Tools and preparation

What you need depends on which parts you're preparing yourself.

For everyone assembling

  • Tweezers - helpful for grabbing and positioning the string and image paper in tight spots.

If you printed the parts yourself

  • Pliers - for removing the upper built-in supports.
  • Small flush cutters - a short, angled pair is strongly preferred for removing the lower supports.
  • Gel super glue - for attaching the lid to the weight carriage.

Assembly Kit parts arrive cleaned, and the weight-carriage lid is already glued.

If you're preparing your own images

  • Single-hole punch - handy for making the paper slot in the Cover Image.

If you choose printed-and-cut images, you do not need a hole punch.

How it works

Two printed images sit inside one physical frame - along with a tiny clock mechanism, a bit of thread, a trap door, and a falling weight. When the time is up, the frame reveals the hidden image.

How exactly does it work?

Simple: a clock winds a string, which pulls a zipper, which releases a latch, which drops a weight, which yoinks a photo, which reveals another.

Will I enjoy putting this together?

That depends almost entirely on what kind of project you enjoy.

This is a miniature machine disguised as a gift: part assembly project, part puzzle, and part experiment. If you enjoy examining unfamiliar parts, understanding how they interact, and making small adjustments until a mechanism comes to life, it is probably your kind of fun.

No individual step is especially difficult. But if you want a quick, strictly linear project that never asks you to stop and think, it may be more aggravating than enjoyable. The build rewards curiosity, patience, and a willingness to tinker.

Can it count down while it ships?

Yes. It is designed to keep counting through normal packing, shipping, jostling, tilting, and being turned upside down. It only needs to be upright when the timer finishes so gravity can do its job.

Does it make noise?

A little. The clock mechanism ticks softly, but unless your recipient is already inspecting the frame for tiny hidden machinery, they are unlikely to notice.

The reveal makes a small thud when the weight drops. A small piece of fabric or felt at the bottom helps muffle it.

Inside the frame

See how the mechanism works

The clock starts a simple chain reaction that removes the cover image.

  • Clock mechanism sits inside the frame.
  • Capstan presses onto the clock mechanism.
  • String ties to the capstan.
    • Then runs through the clock/string guide hole.
    • And through the top-left eyelet.
    • Then ties to the zipper anchor.
  • Zipper head supports the latch.
  • Latch supports the weight.
  • Weight is tethered to the sliding cover image.
  • Cover image sits over the reveal image.
  • When the timer runs, the clock winds the string, pulls the zipper, releases the latch, drops the weight, slides the cover image up, and reveals the image underneath.
